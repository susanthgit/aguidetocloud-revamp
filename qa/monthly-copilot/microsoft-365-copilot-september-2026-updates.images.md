## Resolution upgrade — 14 September 2026 (47 SHAs changed)

A reader complaint about the August issue said images were hard to *read*. Investigation found
two separate causes, both of which silently discarded pixels:

1. **Tech Community URLs carry a server-side resize parameter.** Images were harvested at
   `?image-dimensions=999x694`, which is that site's page-layout width, not the original. Dropping
   the parameter returns the native asset — commonly 2400–3200 px wide, up to 4255 px. Measured
   mean gain across the re-fetched set: **2.53× linear**. 18 official images were re-fetched.
2. **My own generator was halving its output.** `make-illustrations.mjs` renders at a 1200 px
   viewport with `deviceScaleFactor: 2` (native 2400 px) and then resized to 1200 px. The blog's
   content column measures **720 CSS px**, so 1200 px is only 1.67× — below a true 2× on any
   retina display. The resize target is now 1600 px at quality 90, still a downscale from native.
   All 29 diagrams were regenerated; the render viewport is unchanged, so **layout is identical**.

**The observations below were not rewritten, because the depicted content did not change** — only
the pixel count did. That was verified, not assumed:

- Each re-fetched official image was compared per-pixel (NumPy mean absolute difference, new
  image resampled to the audited size) against the originally-audited source. 16 of 18 scored
  1.5–3.3 on a 0–255 scale. Two outliers were investigated rather than waved through:
  `official-62` proved identical (uniform 1.5 across all bands) and `official-24` differs only in
  its top and bottom crop margin (13.6 / 7.6 at the extreme bands, ~1.9 everywhere else).
- One regression was caught and reverted this way: `official-63-agent-harness-choice` was already
  2000 px, and a blanket 1600 px cap had *shrunk* it. It is now stored at its native 4255×1806.
- The regenerated diagrams were re-viewed at display size to confirm they render complete.

Images Microsoft itself published small (for example `official-58` at 507×502) were left alone —
four URL variants including the bare URL all return the same size, so that is the true ceiling,
not a loss on our side.
