## First, the Naming Trap

There is **no Microsoft 365 F5 plan**. This catches people out constantly, because E5 is a full plan and everyone reasonably assumes F5 works the same way.

It doesn't. **F5 is an add-on**, not a base licence. You buy [F1 or F3](/licensing/microsoft-365-frontline/) first, then layer F5 on top of the seats that need it.

> **💡 Plain English:** E5 is a meal. F5 is a side you order with your F1 or F3.

## The Three Options

| Add-on | Price | What It Covers |
|--------|:-----:|---------------|
| **F5 Security** | about $8/user/mo | Identity and threat protection |
| **F5 Compliance** | about $8/user/mo | Data governance and eDiscovery |
| **F5 Security + Compliance** | about $13/user/mo | Both — cheaper than buying them separately |

⚠️ **A note on these prices.** They come from Microsoft's [February 2021 announcement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/licensing/news/new_f5_security_and_compliance_offer_for_frontline_workers), which is the last time Microsoft published F5 add-on pricing publicly. F1 and F3 both went up in July 2026, so the F5 add-ons may well have moved too. Use these as a starting point for a conversation, not a quote.

Buying both separately is $16, so the bundle saves $3 per user per month. If you genuinely only need one side, buy that one.

## What F5 Security Adds

- **Entra ID P2** — Privileged Identity Management, risk-based conditional access, access reviews
- **Defender for Endpoint** — endpoint detection and response on frontline devices
- **Defender for Office** — phishing and malware protection on frontline mailboxes

## What F5 Compliance Adds

- **Advanced data loss prevention** — policies that follow the data
- **eDiscovery** — legal hold and investigation
- **Insider risk management** — detecting risky internal behaviour
- **Information protection** — sensitivity labels and encryption

## Who Actually Needs It

F5 is aimed at frontline environments where the risk profile doesn't match the licence tier — healthcare, finance, government, and anywhere handling regulated data on shared or mobile devices.

The common pattern: a retailer runs F1 for shop floor staff and adds F5 Security only for pharmacy or payments staff. You don't need to buy it for every frontline seat.

> **⚠️ Prerequisite:** F5 add-ons require a Microsoft 365 [F1 or F3](/licensing/microsoft-365-frontline/) base licence. They cannot be bought standalone.

## The Agent 365 Connection

If you're deploying [Agent 365](/licensing/agent-365/) across a frontline workforce, this matters. Agent 365 expects a security baseline underneath it — [Microsoft 365 E5](/licensing/microsoft-365-e5/) for enterprise seats, Business Premium for small business, and the Defender plus Purview coverage in F5 for frontline seats.

## Frequently Asked Questions


**1. Is there a Microsoft 365 F5 plan?**

No. Unlike [E5](/licensing/microsoft-365-e5/), there is no F5 base plan. F5 exists only as add-ons that layer on top of a Microsoft 365 [F1 or F3](/licensing/microsoft-365-frontline/) licence — F5 Security, F5 Compliance, or the two bundled together.



**2. How much do the F5 add-ons cost?**

Microsoft's published list prices are F5 Security $8, F5 Compliance $8, and the bundle $13 per user per month — but those come from the February 2021 announcement, which is the last time Microsoft published them publicly. F1 and F3 both rose in July 2026, so treat these as a starting point and confirm current figures with your reseller. All three are add-ons and require an F1 or F3 base licence.



**3. What does F5 Security add?**

Advanced identity and threat protection for frontline seats — Entra ID P2 (including Privileged Identity Management and risk-based conditional access), Defender for Endpoint, and Defender for Office.



**4. What does F5 Compliance add?**

The data governance side — advanced data loss prevention, eDiscovery, insider risk management, and information protection, bringing frontline seats close to E5 compliance coverage.



**5. Do frontline workers need F5 for Agent 365?**

[Agent 365](/licensing/agent-365/) requires a qualifying security baseline. For enterprise seats that is [Microsoft 365 E5](/licensing/microsoft-365-e5/), and for small business it is Business Premium. For frontline seats, the Defender and Purview coverage in the F5 add-ons is the equivalent baseline.
