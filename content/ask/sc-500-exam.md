## The question

I purchased the SC-500 exam, but I couldn’t find a portal where I can redeem the code I received. Could you please help me with this?

## The answer

Hi Kerim, sorry the activation wasn't clear, and thank you for buying the SC-500 bank. There's no separate portal or login to hunt for.

Your licence key arrives in its own email from **Guided** (email removed), subject starting **"Your licence key for…"**. It is not in the Stripe receipt. The key looks like `GD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`.

Then open the [SC-500 practice page](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/sc-500/). Just below the Flashcards row, find the box marked **"Already purchased?"** and click **Activate licence key**. Paste the key, click **Activate**, and you'll see a green "All questions unlocked" row.

Two walkthroughs show every step with screenshots, for desktop and phone:

- [How to unlock your practice exam](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/activation-guide/)
- [The help page](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/help/)

Still stuck? Email email removed from the address you paid with and I'll sort it out. Please don't paste your key here, it's private to you.

— Sush
