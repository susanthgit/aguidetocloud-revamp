## The question

It will be good to have if you include some guides for SharePoint Advanced Management

## The answer

Hi Deepak — great suggestion, thanks for taking the time.

Honest framing: **SharePoint Advanced Management (SAM)** is a licensed governance add-on, not its own certification — so it doesn't get a standalone cert guide. But it's genuinely useful for Copilot-readiness and oversharing cleanup, which I do cover. Closest resources:

- **[Microsoft's official SAM documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/advanced-management)** — the authoritative reference (Restricted Access Control, Data Access Governance, restricted content discovery, site lifecycle).
- **[Tackling SharePoint oversharing for Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/sharepoint-oversharing-controls-microsoft-365-copilot/)** and **[the Copilot control-system guide](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/microsoft-365-copilot-control-system-complete-guide/)** — my adjacent write-ups on using these controls in practice.

On the cert side: **[SC-401](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/sc-401/)** and **[MS-102](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/ms-102/)** give you the *surrounding* governance knowledge, though neither actually tests SAM itself.

If you tell me what you're using SAM for — pre-Copilot cleanup, Data Access Governance, or lifecycle — I'll look at a focused walkthrough. 🙂

— Sush
