## The question

If we buy, how long is it going to be valid? Since there is no login, do I need to enter the key every time I open site? From laptop or mobile

## The answer

Great questions — here's exactly how it works, no fine print:

- **Validity:** each practice-exam licence is **1 year from purchase**. The free study guide is free forever — no key needed.
- **Re-enter the key every time?** No. You enter your key (`GD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) **once per browser** and it's remembered there until it expires — on your usual laptop you just show up and start.
- **Laptop *and* mobile?** Yes — the key covers **up to 3 activations**, so activate it on your laptop and again on your phone. One honest heads-up: an "activation" is tied to the browser + network, so switching browsers or networks can occasionally use one up. If you ever run out, just reply to the email your key came in and I'll reset it for you.
- **No login?** Correct — there are no accounts; the key *is* your access, so keep that email. And avoid private/incognito windows (they wipe on close).

— Sush
