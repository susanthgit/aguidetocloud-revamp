## The question

Hi there,

How can I download all the questions with answers for AI-100? I study way better on paper. Thank you.

Kind regards,
Craig

## The answer

Hi Craig — thanks for reaching out, and I feel you on studying from paper.

Quick heads-up first: **AI-100 was retired by Microsoft** a while back, so there's no current AI-100 set. Depending on which you meant:

- If you meant the old **AI-100** (AI engineer), the current path is **[AI-103 — Azure AI App & Agent Developer](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/ai-103/)**.
- If you actually meant **AI-900** (fundamentals), that's now **[AI-901 — Azure AI Fundamentals](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/ai-901/)**.

On paper: the practice questions run in an interactive engine (instant explanations, timed mode), so there's no bulk PDF of the bank — but the **full study guide is free and 100% readable**, so open it and use your browser's **Print / Save as PDF** for a copy to mark up.

Tell me your background (developer vs brand-new to AI) and which of the two you're aiming at, and I'll point you to the right one. 🙂

— Sush
