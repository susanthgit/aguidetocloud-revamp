## The question

Hello Sush,
first of all my personal congrats for A Guide to Cloud & AI.
Great work, really impressive!

I would like to ask if by any chance you are planning to publish 2 new videos on the cert AI-901: Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals.
By the way, FYI, the corresponding guide still displays it to be in Beta version, but actually AI-901 has now officially replaced the AI-900, permanently retired.

Furthermore, I would be interested in additional support material & questions about AI models and agents built with Microsoft Foundry.
I guess it's gonna be a hot topic quite soon, so please take it more as an advice rather than a request.

Thanks again for your relentless efforts
Cheers
alek

## The answer

Hi Alek — thank you, that genuinely means a lot.

**You're right about the beta label, and thanks for flagging it.** AI-901 went GA and AI-900 retired on 30 June 2026, but the cert tracker was still showing AI-901 as a beta exam — including an 80% discount banner that hasn't been true for months. That's the kind of stale detail that costs someone real money, so I'm glad you said something.

It's fixed now, and it turned out to be bigger than the label. Digging into it, the script that builds the tracker had a broken URL that was silently dropping three other exams (DP-600, DP-700, MB-820) off the index completely. Those are back too. So your one comment fixed four things.

**On the two AI-901 videos** — honest answer: I'd rather not promise a date I can't keep. I want to make them, and if I get the time I will, but I can't commit to when. Better to tell you that than give you a date that slips.

**On Foundry** — you're right that it's the hot topic, and it's actually where the exam has moved. AI-901 is just two domains now, and *Implement AI solutions by using Microsoft Foundry* is 55-60% of the whole thing. There's more there already than you might expect:

- The [AI-901 study guide](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/ai-901/) — 26 modules, 15 of them on Foundry: the SDK, agents, Content Understanding, the portal
- The [AI-901 practice exam](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/ai-901/) — 20 free questions, 265 in the full bank, split across both domains

If you work through those and still hit gaps on the agents side, tell me exactly where. That's genuinely useful for deciding what to build next.

Cheers, and thanks again for flagging the beta thing.
Sush
