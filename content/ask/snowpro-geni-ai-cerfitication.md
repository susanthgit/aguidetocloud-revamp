## The question

I’m preparing for the SnowPro Specialty Gen AI (GES-C02) certification. If I thoroughly practice all 250 questions from the **A Guide to Cloud** practice test and understand the answers, how much does it improve my chances of clearing the actual certification exam? Is this practice test recommended?

## The answer

Hi Avinash, understanding the answers is the right approach. I can't give you a reliable percentage, or promise a pass from completing the 250 questions alone.

Use them to find the gaps in your understanding. For each question, try to explain why the correct answer is right and why the others are wrong, then practise that topic in Snowflake itself.

Pair them with the [official GES-C02 objectives and study guide](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/certifications/snowpro-GenAI-C02/). Snowflake describes the target candidate as having a year or more of hands-on Gen AI experience on Snowflake, plus Python and SQL, so lean on real practice alongside the questions. These are original practice questions written for revision, not real exam items.

— Sush
