## The question

Big fan of the work you're putting together... Would you provide PS for AI implementation?

## The answer

Hi Fabiano — thank you, that genuinely means a lot. 🙏

Honest answer: I don't offer paid professional services or hands-on implementation — A Guide to Cloud is an education-first project (my nights-and-weekends way of sharing what I learn), not a consulting shop. So I can't take on an implementation engagement itself.

What I *can* do, and am always happy to:
- **Point you the right way.** If you have specific questions about implementing Copilot or AI in your environment, ask them here or through the feedback form and I'll do my best to help.
- **Save you the legwork.** A lot of the site is built to be a practical, do-it-yourself implementation guide. Good starting points:
  → [Microsoft 365 Copilot deployment — best-practices checklist](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/microsoft-365-copilot-deployment-best-practices-ultimate-checklist/)
  → [Microsoft's Copilot adoption hub](https://adoption.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/) (free rollout playbooks, scenario library, success kit)

If you're after hands-on help to actually deliver it, the best route is a **Microsoft partner** who specialises in Copilot/AI — Microsoft's [partner finder](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/solution-providers/home) lets you search by specialty and region.

Really glad the work is useful to you — and keep the questions coming.

— Sush
