## The answer

Hi Stefan — thanks for the kind words and for sharing those references. I’ve updated the [Education licensing guide](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/licensing/microsoft-365-education/) and the Licensing Simplifier with the A7 Student Use Benefit details I could verify in [Microsoft’s Product Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/StudentUseBenefitsandAcademicPrograms/all).

For Microsoft 365 Education A7, Microsoft lists Office 365 A5 Student Use Benefit, Enterprise Mobility + Security A5 Student Use Benefit, Windows Education A3 Student Use Benefit, and Minecraft: Education Edition. The terms list EES and CSP availability and 40 student instances per qualifying paid Faculty/Staff, Education Qualified User, or Knowledge Worker licence.

On the Defender question: the public terms list those student-use products, but don’t itemize the Defender components or confirm that students receive the same Defender suite as faculty. I’ve made that limitation clear rather than guessing; the institution’s agreement or Microsoft licensing provider is the source for the exact student-use rights. Microsoft’s public Education pages also don’t list A3/A5/A7 dollar prices, so the guide points readers to request a current quote.

Thanks again — your feedback helped us replace outdated pricing and explain what Microsoft’s public terms do and don’t establish.
