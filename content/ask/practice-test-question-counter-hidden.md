## The question

I'm on a 50-question practice test but I don't the stage I'm in

## The answer

Hi — thanks for flagging this. You found a real bug, not a missing feature, and you're the first person to spot it.

**Short answer: the counter was always there, you just couldn't see it.**

In fullscreen the quiz has a toolbar across the top showing `Q 4/50`, a progress bar, your timer, your running score and the Exit button. On desktop that whole toolbar was being drawn *underneath* the site's navigation bar, so it was completely invisible. On a phone you'd have seen your position at the bottom of the screen; on a laptop you'd have seen nothing at all. So "I don't know the stage I'm in" was exactly right — there was genuinely nothing on screen telling you.

**The cause was mine, and it was a layering mistake.** The fullscreen quiz panel sat on a lower stacking layer than the site header, so the header painted straight over the top of it. The quiz's own toolbar never stood a chance — it was trapped underneath its own container. Fixed and deployed today.

Hard-refresh the practice page (**Ctrl+Shift+R**, or **Cmd+Shift+R** on a Mac) and you should now see, across the top:

- `Q 4/50` — where you are in the run
- a progress bar
- your timer
- your running score
- the Exit button

Two things came out of it that I'd rather tell you than quietly fix. The **five-minute warning** was being hidden by exactly the same bug, so that's back as well. And I've added an automated check that now runs before every release — if that toolbar is ever covered again, the build fails instead of shipping. This one survived as long as it did precisely because nothing was watching for it.

**One question back to you**, because your wording could mean two different things:

1. **The position indicator at the top** — that's what's now fixed, or
2. **A number on the question card itself** — e.g. "Question 4 of 50" sitting just above the question text.

If you meant 2, or you'd like both, say so and I'll add it. It's a small change and it's a fair ask.

One last thing, in case it looks like a second bug: **Question Map · 3/50 answered** counts the questions you've *answered*, not where you are. If you skip ahead with Next it deliberately won't move. The `Q 4/50` counter is the one that tracks your position.

Thanks again for taking the time to write it up.

— Sush
