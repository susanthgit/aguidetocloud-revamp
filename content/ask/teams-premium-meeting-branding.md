## The question

If I want my organizations users when they do meetings via MS teams their MS teams background will be with our company logo and branding also,, how can I achieve this?

## The answer

Hi Ahmed — great question. Putting your company logo and branding on your users' Teams meetings is a **Teams Premium** feature (**meeting customization / branding**), so the first requirement is that each user who organises branded meetings has a **Teams Premium** licence.

Then, as a Teams admin:
1. **Teams admin center → Meetings → Customization policies** (also called *meeting branding*).
2. Upload your **logo**, a **brand/background image**, and set your **brand colours**.
3. **Assign** the policy to the users or groups who should get it.

That brands the join screen, lobby, and meeting backgrounds with your logo across the org. Note it's **Teams Premium only** — it won't appear for standard-licence users. Microsoft's [custom meeting branding docs](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoftteams/custom-meeting-branding) have the exact click-path. Hope that helps! 🙂
