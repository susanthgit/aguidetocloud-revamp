## The question

Kalen has provided me with the full co-pilot license but I don't see where I log in and/or access the more powerful features beyond the basic copilot access

## The answer

Hi Jeff — great question, and congrats on the full licence. This one trips a lot of people up, because the upgrade doesn't announce itself — it just quietly unlocks more.

Here's where to find it:

**1. Sign in with your *work* account.** The full experience lives in the **Microsoft 365 Copilot app** — open it at **[m365.cloud.microsoft](https://m365.cloud.microsoft)**, or install the "Microsoft 365 Copilot" app on Windows, in Teams, or on your phone. Make sure you're signed in with the work account Kalen licensed — *not* a personal Microsoft account. This is where Researcher, Analyst, Notebooks and the agents live.

**2. Look for the Copilot button inside your apps.** Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams each have a Copilot button on the Home ribbon (desktop or web), as long as you're signed in with the licensed account.

**3. What actually changed.** The big upgrade is **work grounding** — Copilot can now securely use *your* emails, files, chats and meetings (through Microsoft Graph) to answer, on top of the web. That's the "more powerful" part you're after.

**If it still looks basic:** licence changes can take **up to ~24 hours** to fully light up. Sign out and back in, restart the apps, confirm you're on the latest version, and if it's been a day, ask Kalen to double-check the licence shows as *assigned* to your account.
