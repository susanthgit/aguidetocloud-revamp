## The question

I bought a practice exam and got my licence key by email. The email told me to enter the key in the "orange activation box" on the practice page.

I can't find any orange box on the page. Where do I actually enter the key?

## The answer

Short version: **there is no orange box on the website.** That wording in our purchase email was our mistake. It's now fixed — sorry for the confusion.

The orange box is in the **email**, and it's simply where your key is *printed*. On the practice page, look for the **"Already purchased?"** panel with an **"Activate licence key"** button. (This used to be a small, easy-to-miss link — we replaced it on 25 August 2026.)

Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

---

### 1. Find your key

Your key arrives in **its own email — not the Stripe receipt**. The Stripe receipt is just proof of payment and never contains the key.

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | `email removed` (sender name: **Guided**) |
| **Subject** | **Your licence key for …** — e.g. *"Your licence key for AI-200 Practice Exam — 1 Year Access"* |
| **Look for** | An orange panel headed **YOUR LICENCE KEY** |

The key looks like `GD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`.

Can't find it? Search your mail for `from:aguidetocloud.com`, and check **Spam** and (in Gmail) the **Promotions** tab.

---

### 2. You may already be unlocked

If you're using **the same browser you bought it in**, you're unlocked automatically — you don't need the key at all. Look for the green **"All &lt;N&gt; questions unlocked"** row.

You only need to type the key in when you're on **another device or browser**, or if you cleared your browser data.

---

### 3. Open the practice page

Use the **Start Practicing →** button in that same email, or go to your exam page and open **Practice**.

---

### 4. Click the activation link

It's a small line sitting **above Quiz Options** (just under the Flashcards row, when that's shown). The coloured words are the button — click those.

---

### 5. Paste the key, click Activate

Paste it straight from the email rather than retyping. Then you'll see **✓ All &lt;N&gt; questions unlocked**, and the Domain dropdown jumps to the full question count.

> **The "Unlock All — $9" button stays on the page. That's normal.**
> It's part of the page header and doesn't disappear after you activate — it does **not** mean activation failed. The green row is the real confirmation.

---

### If something doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"I can't find the orange box"** | There isn't one on the site. The orange box is in your *email* — it's where the key is shown. On the site, look for the **"Already purchased?"** panel with the **"Activate licence key"** button, above **Quiz Options**. |
| **"Licence key not found. Please check and try again."** | Almost always a typo. Copy and paste straight from the email, and watch for a trailing space. |
| **"Invalid licence key format. Expected: GD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX"** | Paste the whole key including the `GD-` prefix — three groups of four after it. |
| **"This key has reached its 3-device limit."** | Each key covers 3 devices. Email us and we'll reset it. |
| **"This licence key has expired."** | Access runs 12 months from purchase. Email us if that looks wrong. |
| **I don't see the activation link at all** | It's hidden when you're **already unlocked** in that browser — look for the green "All &lt;N&gt; questions unlocked" row instead. It's also hidden on free exams (like AZ-900), which need no key. |
| **It worked yesterday, now I'm locked again** | Access is stored in your browser, so clearing site data or using a **private/incognito** window wipes it. Just re-enter the key — usually this won't use up one of your 3, because we recognise the same device. |
| **I want it on my laptop *and* my phone** | Enter the same key on each. You get 3 devices. |
| **I paid but never got the email** | Check Spam and Gmail's Promotions tab, and search `from:aguidetocloud.com`. Still nothing — email us from the address you paid with and we'll resend it. |

---

### Good to know

- **No account, no password.** Your licence key *is* your access — keep the email and you can restore it any time.
- **3 devices**, **12 months** from purchase.
- Still stuck? Email **email removed** from the address you paid with.
