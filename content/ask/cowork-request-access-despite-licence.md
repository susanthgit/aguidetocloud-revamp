## The question

I am the administrator.  I have assigned Copilot for Business licenses to two users.  When they get into Cowork, they are prompted to request access.   I can see those requests in the Portal > Copilot > Cowork section.  When I click on View Requests, it takes me to the User page, and there's an alert on the top of the page to View request.  So far so good.

When I click that, it simply takes me to the licensing page, but my users already have Copliot for Business.  There's nothing to add.  What else do they need?

## The answer

Hi Denis — thanks for the detailed write-up, that really helps.

Short version: this isn't a licensing gap. Your two users are licensed fine — what they're actually hitting is Cowork's **usage-based billing**. Cowork needs two things: a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence (✓ you've done that) **and** the users added to a **Copilot Credits spending policy**. The "request access" prompt is a *credit* request, not a licence one — which is exactly why the licensing page has nothing to add.

**Where to approve it:** Microsoft 365 admin center → **Copilot → Cost Management → Overview → Top actions → View requests**. That "Credit requests" page shows each user's licence status and current policy, and lets you add them to a spending policy (create one first if you haven't set up usage-based billing yet).

- Microsoft — [the discovery setting](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/discovery-setting-ai-experiences)

Hope that unblocks them!
