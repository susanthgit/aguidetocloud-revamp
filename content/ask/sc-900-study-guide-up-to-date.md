## The question

Thank you for the content. I appreciate the material provided.

Could you please confirm whether this content has been updated as of July 2026? I noticed that the module indicates the last update was in May 2025, so I wanted to verify if there have been any revisions or newer versions since then.

## The answer

Hi Kaustav — thanks for the kind words, and good on you for checking the date rather than assuming.

On the content itself: the SC-900 study material I reviewed maps to the **current exam outline** — it reflects Microsoft's **November 2025 SC-900 skills update** (Defender XDR consolidation, Copilot for Security, the Purview rebranding). So you're studying against the live objectives.

On the **"May 2025" you saw** — I haven't been able to reproduce that label on my end, so I don't want to hand-wave it. Could you share the **exact page URL** (or a screenshot) where it shows? If it's a stale stamp I'll correct it; if it's pointing at something genuinely older, I want to know so I can fix the actual content.

Either way, you can sanity-check against Microsoft's official [SC-900 skills outline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-900/) — the [free guide](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/sc-900/) is mapped to it and the [practice bank](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/sc-900/) covers all 4 domains.

— Sush
