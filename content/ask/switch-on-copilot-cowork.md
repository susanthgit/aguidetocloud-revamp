## The question

Hey there - LOVE your site - it is so practical and helps me so much! Now having set that, I am finding it challenging to switch on Co-work for our organisation. Credits are purchased, and I can see a request, and yet the 'on' button to make it visible eludes. Thank you for your insight, Judith

## The answer

Hi Judith — thank you, that's really kind and I'm so glad the site helps. 🙏

The "on" switch is in a slightly different place than you'd expect. At general availability, Cowork sits behind **two** gates — having the credits is only half of it:

**1. Turn on visibility.** Microsoft 365 admin center → **Copilot → Settings** → switch on **"AI experiences enabled by usage-based billing."** It's off by default, and it's the setting that actually makes Cowork *appear* for users.

**2. Add people to a spending policy.** **Copilot → Cost Management** → create a spending policy that includes the users you want on Cowork. This is what lets them actually *use* it — and adding someone to a policy also makes Cowork show up for them even if the first toggle is still off.

That "request" you can see is almost certainly a user asking for access from inside Cowork — once they're covered by a spending policy, that's what grants it.

One tip: after flipping these on, give it a little time to propagate before worrying — tenant changes aren't always instant.

I've written up the full enablement + troubleshooting path here, including a section called *"Why a user can't see Cowork"* that runs through the five usual causes:
→ [Copilot Cowork admin & governance guide — "Why a user can't see Cowork"](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-cowork-admin-and-governance/#why-a-user-cant-see-cowork-in-microsoft-365-copilot)

And Microsoft's own doc on that exact setting:
→ [Manage the AI experiences setting (Microsoft Learn)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/discovery-setting-ai-experiences)
