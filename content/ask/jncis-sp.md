## The question

Does your JNCIS-SP cover the new exam code JN0-364?

https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/juniper-jncis-sp/

## The answer

Thanks for checking the version before relying on it. That's exactly the right instinct.

Short answer: the practice bank is built around Juniper's current **JN0-364** objectives, on **Junos OS 25.2**, and the 250 questions cover all ten topic areas (protocol-independent routing, OSPF, IS-IS, BGP, Layer 2 and VLANs, spanning tree, MPLS, IPv6, tunnels, and high availability). So yes, it lines up with JN0-364.

One honest caveat: these are my own practice questions, not real exam items, so I can't promise every one matches the exact phrasing or depth of the live exam. Use them to find your weak spots, then confirm against Juniper's [official objectives](https://learningportal.juniper.net/juniper/user_activity_info.aspx?id=14325).

You also helped me catch two things on my own page, which I'm fixing now: it called JNCIA-Junos "recommended" when Juniper actually requires it, and it didn't clearly show the JN0-364 code or the Junos 25.2 version. Thank you for the nudge.

— Sush
