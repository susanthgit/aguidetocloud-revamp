## The answer

Hi — thanks for flagging this, you're right that it's worth clarifying. And good news first: you've found it. 🎉

Cowork shows up in a couple of spots depending on your app version — the **left rail** is one of them, and it also lives in **Outlook and Teams** now. Microsoft's own steps point to the toggle next to Chat, but the Microsoft 365 app is rolling out a new layout where Cowork sits in the **left navigation rail** too — so both are valid, and what you're seeing is expected.

Where to find it:
- Our [step-by-step Cowork guide](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-cowork-how-to-use-step-by-step/)
- Microsoft's official [Get started with Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/get-started)

I've updated our guide to call out the left-rail entry point too — thanks for the nudge. 🙏
