## The question

I can find any way to configure the enterprise brand manager policy in the M365 Apps Admin Center, is that feature still in private preview?

## The answer

Dean — great spot, and thanks for flagging this. You're not missing anything obvious in the UI — there are real prerequisites that get tripped up.

The Enterprise Brand Manager policy isn't in the M365 Apps Admin Center menu itself — it's configured over at **config.office.com** (Microsoft 365 Apps Admin Center → Customisation), but it only surfaces once a few prerequisites are in place:

1. A **SharePoint Organizational Asset Library** is set up for templates and images
2. A **Mail-Enabled Security Group** exists for your brand managers
3. Your tenant is on the right release channel — the PowerPoint Agent / Brand Kit experience is in **Insiders Beta Channel** as of April 2026 and rolling out more broadly. If you're on Monthly Enterprise or Semi-Annual, the policy surface might not be visible yet.

I wrote a full walkthrough of all this — including the policy setup, the gotchas, and what Brand Kit actually does once it's running — here: [**M365 Copilot Brand Kit — Setup Guide for IT Admins**](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/blog/microsoft-365-copilot-brand-kit-complete-guide/)

If you go through it and still hit a wall on a specific step, reply here and I'll dig deeper with you.

— Sush
