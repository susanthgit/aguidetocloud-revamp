## About JNCIS-DC

JNCIS-DC is the specialist credential in Juniper's Data Center track. Exam **JN0-481** covers data center fabric concepts and the design, deployment and operation of Juniper Apstra. Juniper lists Apstra 5.1 and Junos 24.4 as the exam software versions.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The certification is for data center networking professionals with intermediate knowledge of Apstra and data center devices. **JNCIA-DC is a prerequisite.**

## Study Tips

1. Start with spine-leaf fabrics, EVPN and VXLAN before working through Apstra's design and deployment stages.
2. Use the [Apstra 5.1 user guide](https://www.juniper.net/documentation/us/en/software/apstra5.1/apstra-user-guide/index.html) to review blueprint operations, multitenancy and intent-based analytics.
