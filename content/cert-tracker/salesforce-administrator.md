## About the Salesforce Platform Administrator Exam

> Master the Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator exam: decide between a profile and a permission set, open up record access without breaking the sharing model, choose the right automation tool for a scenario, judge what actually breaks when a field is deleted, import and de-duplicate data safely, build reports and dashboards that respect folder sharing, and configure and troubleshoot Agentforce agents and their permissions.

The complete 250-question practice exam for the Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator credential, written to the eight-domain blueprint Salesforce published in its December 2025 exam refresh. Covers configuration and setup including company settings, fiscal year and business hours, declarative user interface administration, user setup and licence distribution, why administrators deactivate rather than delete a user, organization security controls such as identity verification, login hours, session settings and the Setup Audit Trail, the sharing model end to end from org-wide defaults through role hierarchy, public groups, sharing rules and manual sharing, and the decision between a custom profile, a permission set, a permission set group and muting; object manager and Lightning App Builder including standard object architecture, lookup, master-detail and junction relationships, record types and business processes, Schema Builder, creating and customizing fields and page layouts, and the real implications of deleting a field that participates in a roll-up summary, a cross-object formula or a picklist dependency; sales and marketing applications including the lead and opportunity lifecycle, lead conversion, lead and campaign management, assignment rules, path, forecasting, territory management and Einstein for Sales; service and support applications including case management, queues, assignment and escalation rules, auto-response rules, support processes and Einstein for Service; productivity and collaboration including activity management, Chatter groups and internal versus external access, the Salesforce mobile app, and AppExchange managed and unmanaged packages; data and analytics management including Data Import Wizard versus Data Loader, mass transfer, mass delete, export and backup, duplicate and matching rules, validation rules, report types and joined reports, bucketing, grouping and summary formulas, how the sharing model and folder structure affect report visibility, and dashboard data sources, dynamic dashboards, running user, subscriptions and refresh scheduling; automation including choosing the right tool for a scenario, screen flows, record-triggered flows and autolaunched flows, the order of execution, and approval processes with their entry criteria, approvers and rejection paths; and Agentforce including appropriate use cases for an agent, agent security and permissions, troubleshooting agent access, and maintaining topics, instructions and prompts in Agent Builder with conversation preview and testing. Original practice questions.
