## About the NVIDIA NCP-ARI Exam

> NCP-ARI focuses on the physical work that makes an AI installation ready: racks, power, cooling interfaces, cables, supports and acceptance evidence.

The **NVIDIA-Certified Professional: AI Rack and Interconnect (NCP-ARI)** certification is aimed at data center technicians working with high-density AI infrastructure. NVIDIA's current exam page lists **70 questions**, a **120-minute** duration and a **US$400** exam fee.

This independent bank contains **250 original single-answer practice questions**. It tests decisions around physical installation, not manual dexterity or permission to perform electrical, structural or cooling-system work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA and contains no official or recalled exam questions.

**Before you buy:** NVIDIA's current Domain 5 objectives include two unrelated OpenUSD bullets about unexpected visual results and updating a mesh's extent. This bank **does not cover those two bullets**. It covers the four physical cable-support objectives in that domain and does not claim complete coverage of every published bullet. The overview also mentions liquid-cooling manifolds and DC busbar power; questions address supported facility-interface and readiness decisions without inventing installation procedures.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

NCP-ARI is relevant to data center infrastructure technicians, hardware installation teams, physical-network technicians and facilities engineers preparing high-density NVIDIA environments.

NVIDIA recommends **2+ years of enterprise data center operations experience**, particularly with high-density computing or supercomputing infrastructure. Study questions complement that experience; they do not replace supervised practical training, an approved installation plan or qualified personnel.

## NVIDIA Certification Path

NCP-ARI concerns the **physical installation**: rack readiness, facility interfaces, high-density cabling and safe handover. [NCP-AII](/cert-tracker/nvidia-ncp-aii/) covers broader AI infrastructure deployment and validation, while [NCP-AIN](/cert-tracker/nvidia-ncp-ain/) covers fabric configuration and operation.

Those subjects overlap in a real data center, but they are separate exams. Use the current objectives for the exam you plan to sit rather than assuming one bank is interchangeable with another.

## Study Tips

1. **Keep the platform attached to the procedure.** An NDR cabling guide is not a universal XDR rule, and a rack's clearance, power and cooling requirements come from its actual equipment documentation.
2. **Check the failure condition.** Two power cords do not establish redundancy unless the remaining feed can support the stated load.
3. **Separate physical compatibility from appearance.** Connector shells, fiber type, polish, lane mapping and supported part numbers all matter.
4. **Treat link-up as one piece of evidence.** Verify the intended width, rate and signal quality before accepting a cable installation.
5. **Preserve serviceability.** Cable routes must protect airflow, bend radius, strain relief, identification and access for maintenance.
6. **Keep work within authorization.** Electrical isolation, grounding, lifting and structural or cooling work require qualified personnel and site procedures. A practice answer is not permission to perform hazardous work.
7. **Check the current source.** Read the [official NCP-ARI objectives](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/ai-rack-and-interconnect-professional/) and the [NVIDIA cabling design guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx-superpod/design-guide-cabling-data-centers/latest/index.html) alongside the exact manuals for your installation.
