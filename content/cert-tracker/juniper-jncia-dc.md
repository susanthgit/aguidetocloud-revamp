## About the JNCIA-DC Exam

JNCIA-DC is the associate-level certification in the Juniper data center track. The JN0-281 exam covers data center networking concepts and introductory configuration and troubleshooting on Junos OS 24.2.

This independent study guide and practice bank are not affiliated with or endorsed by Juniper Networks or HPE.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The exam is intended for data center networking professionals with introductory knowledge of Junos software and data center devices. There is no prerequisite certification.

## Study Tips

1. Compare traditional and spine-leaf designs, and distinguish the underlay from the overlay.
2. Practise VLAN, switching and IRB configuration and troubleshooting in a suitable Junos lab.
3. Review static, aggregate and generated routes alongside OSPF and BGP fundamentals.
4. Study LAG, graceful restart and BFD, then review Virtual Chassis concepts.
5. Use the [official preparation resources](https://learningportal.juniper.net/juniper/user_activity_info.aspx?id=14349) alongside your practice questions.
