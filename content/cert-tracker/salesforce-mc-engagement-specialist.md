## About the Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement Specialist Exam

> Master the Salesforce Certified Marketing Cloud Engagement Specialist exam: build and send an email the right way in Email Studio, choose between a triggered send, a user-initiated send and an automated send, design an Automation Studio automation and know when Journey Builder is the better tool, model subscribers across lists, data extensions and the All Subscribers list, write the SQL query activity that segments them, honour unsubscribe and CAN-SPAM obligations, and read a tracking report well enough to say what actually happened.

The complete 250-question practice exam for the Salesforce Certified Marketing Cloud Engagement Specialist credential, written to the five sections Salesforce publishes in its official exam guide. Covers email marketing best practices including permission-based marketing, CAN-SPAM and commercial-email obligations, unsubscribe and opt-out handling, subscriber preference and profile centres, sender reputation, authentication and deliverability, list hygiene and re-engagement, and the difference between a hard and a soft bounce; content creation and delivery including Content Builder blocks, templates and reusable content, email types and paste-HTML versus template-based builds, dynamic and personalised content with personalisation strings and AMPscript at a working level, GTL and basic SSJS awareness, approvals, testing and previewing against subscriber data, A/B testing, send classifications and delivery profiles, sender profiles, and the mechanics of a user-initiated, triggered, scheduled and automated send; marketing automation including Automation Studio starting sources, file drops and schedules, the SQL query, import, file transfer, data extract, filter and script activities, Journey Builder entry sources, decision splits, waits and goals, when a journey beats an automation, and how the two are combined; subscriber and data management including subscribers, contacts, leads and the All Subscribers list, lists versus data extensions, sendable and non-sendable data extensions, subscriber keys and contact keys, data relationships and data designer, imports, filters and segmentation, data retention, and preference and profile attributes; and insights and analytics including tracking and the standard email tracking metrics, open, click, bounce, unsubscribe and complaint reporting, unique versus total measures, reports and report scheduling, Web and Google Analytics integration, and reading a send's performance to decide what to change next. Questions align to the Summer '23 release, the release Salesforce names in the official exam guide.
