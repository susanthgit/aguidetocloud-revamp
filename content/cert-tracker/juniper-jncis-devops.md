## About the JNCIS-DevOps Exam

JNCIS-DevOps (JN0-423) is Juniper's specialist certification for network automation. The exam covers platform automation, gRPC and telemetry, Ansible, Junos automation scripts, and YANG. Juniper lists Junos OS 24.4 as the exam software baseline.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The certification is intended for networking professionals with intermediate knowledge of automation tools and practices. JNCIA-DevOps is the prerequisite certification. Written exams can be taken in any order, but the prerequisite must be fulfilled to earn the certification.
