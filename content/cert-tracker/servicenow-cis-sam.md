## Who Should Take This Exam?

The CIS-SAM is designed for **implementation specialists who deploy and run ServiceNow Software Asset Management** — the people responsible for software discovery and normalization, license metrics and entitlements, reconciliation and compliance, and the contract, procurement and reclamation processes that sit around them. It assumes you already work in the platform daily rather than studying it from scratch.

**Prerequisites:** Required: CIS - Data Foundations (CMDB and CSDM) must be held before sitting CIS-SAM. ServiceNow also recommends Software Asset Management Fundamentals training and hands-on implementation experience; consult the current ServiceNow registration requirements.

**Typical study time:** 6-10 weeks of focused study

## ServiceNow Certification Path

ServiceNow **does** gate this credential: **CIS - Data Foundations (CMDB and CSDM)** must be held before you sit CIS-SAM. Most candidates reach it by way of the Certified System Administrator (CSA), which establishes platform navigation, the data schema and the CMDB that Software Asset Management builds on. ServiceNow also recommends the Software Asset Management Fundamentals training and hands-on implementation experience — check the current registration requirements before booking, as ServiceNow changes them from time to time.
