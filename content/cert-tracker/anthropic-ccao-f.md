## About the CCAO-F Exam

> Practise everyday decisions about prompting, checking outputs, choosing Claude features, and using them responsibly at work.

This independent bank contains **250 original practice questions** mapped to Anthropic's **Claude Certified Associate - Foundations Exam Guide, version 1.0, July 2026**. It is not official Anthropic exam material, endorsed training, or an exam voucher.

The questions focus on Claude's user-facing products and business workflows. API development and advanced system architecture are outside this Associate bank's scope.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The guide describes business and knowledge-work professionals with limited-to-moderate technical experience. There are no mandatory prerequisites or required courses.

## Study Tips

1. **Start with output evaluation**, the highest-weighted domain at 21%.
2. **Practise in Claude**, using non-sensitive material to try the product behaviors discussed in explanations.
3. **Read why the alternatives are wrong**, not just the correct answer.
4. **Separate an organization's rules from a product's capabilities**; neither grants access to data you are not authorized to use.
5. **Check the current official guide and product documentation**. This bank retains the July 2026 syllabus until a replacement syllabus is published.
