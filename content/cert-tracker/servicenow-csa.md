## Who Should Take This Exam?

The ServiceNow Certified System Administrator is designed for **administrators who configure and support a ServiceNow instance day to day**. ServiceNow does not publish a required amount of hands-on experience; it recommends completing the ServiceNow Administration Fundamentals course before sitting the exam.

**Prerequisites:** None required. ServiceNow recommends completing the ServiceNow Administration Fundamentals course.

**Typical study time:** 4-8 weeks of focused study
