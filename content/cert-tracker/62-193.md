## About the 62-193 Exam

> Master exam 62-193 — the 21st Century Learning Design framework, collaboration, skilled communication, knowledge construction, self-regulation, real-world problem solving, and using ICT to design better learning activities.

The complete practice exam for the Microsoft Certified Educator (MCE) certification, exam 62-193. Built for teachers, teacher-trainees and teacher-educators: you are assessed on how well you design and evaluate learning activities using the 21CLD dimensions — not on whether you can operate a particular piece of software.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The 62-193 is designed for **beginners and career changers**. No prior experience required.

**Prerequisites:** None. Certiport and Microsoft recommend practical classroom teaching experience plus familiarity with the 21st Century Learning Design (21CLD) framework and everyday use of Microsoft 365 tools in teaching.

**Typical study time:** 2-4 weeks of part-time study
