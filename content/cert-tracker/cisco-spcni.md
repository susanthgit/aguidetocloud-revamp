## About the SPCNI Exam

> Design and operate service-provider cloud infrastructure — NFV, cloud interconnect, resilient NFVI, security, assurance, and VNF optimization

The 300-540 SPCNI (Designing and Implementing Cisco Service Provider Cloud Network Infrastructure) is a CCNP Service Provider concentration exam. It validates your ability to work across virtualized architecture and NFV; carrier-neutral facilities and cloud interconnect; NFVI-specific high availability; infrastructure and NFVI security; and service assurance and optimization. The exam includes Cisco and standards-based technologies such as NSO, OpenStack, NETCONF, RESTCONF, YANG, gNMI/gRPC, EVPN-VXLAN, MPLS and Segment Routing handoffs, IPsec, uRPF, RTBH, BGP FlowSpec, MACsec, telemetry, IPFIX, SR-PM, SR-IOV, DPDK, and VPP. Passing SPCNI earns the Cisco Certified Specialist - Service Provider Cloud Network Infrastructure certification and, with the SPCOR 350-501 core exam, can satisfy the concentration requirement for CCNP Service Provider. ## Who Should Take This Exam?

SPCNI is designed for **service-provider cloud network architects, NFV engineers, cloud interconnect engineers, network automation engineers, and operations engineers** who design, implement, or troubleshoot virtualized network infrastructure. There is no formal prerequisite to sit the exam, but practical experience with service-provider routing, data-center interconnect, virtualization, APIs, telemetry, and high-availability design helps a lot.

**Typical study time:** 6-10 weeks of focused study

## Study Tips

1. **Start with the two 25% domains** — together they account for half the blueprint
2. **Keep the NFV roles separate** — NFVI, VIM, VNFM, NFVO, VNF, NSO, and OpenStack are related but not interchangeable
3. **Study interconnect as a boundary problem** — carrier-neutral facilities, cloud-provider attachment, DCI gateways, and encrypted overlays solve different needs
4. **Think in failure domains** — VNF placement alone does not remove shared compute, vNIC, uplink, ToR, state, or control-plane dependencies
5. **Separate protocol depth from the SPCNI decision** — use EVPN, MPLS/SR, uRPF, FlowSpec, and telemetry as evidence in a cloud-infrastructure design or diagnosis
6. **Simulate exam conditions** — use timed Exam Mode after you are comfortable explaining why every distractor is wrong
