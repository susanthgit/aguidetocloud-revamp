## About the AAIR Exam

ISACA Advanced in AI Risk (AAIR) is an advanced credential for experienced professionals managing the risks and opportunities associated with AI. It focuses on governance, lifecycle decisions, risk assessment, treatment, monitoring, suppliers, and resilience.

The practice exam contains 250 original questions, with explanations and scenario-based context across all three domains. It is an independent study resource, not an official ISACA exam or a collection of real exam questions.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

AAIR is aimed at professionals who work across risk, governance, technology, and business teams. Candidates must hold a qualifying designation; ISACA's published list includes several ISACA credentials and other global designations. Check the [current eligibility list](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aair) before registering.

## Study Tips

1. Connect AI use cases and lifecycle risks to the organization's objectives, risk appetite, and accountable owners.
2. Practise assessing scenarios, choosing treatments, evaluating controls, and reporting meaningful risk measures.
3. Consider suppliers, data, model changes, human oversight, and continuity throughout the lifecycle.
4. Separate applicable legal requirements from voluntary guidance, and use the facts given in each scenario.
5. Review the [official exam outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aair/aair-exam-content-outline) and the [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).
