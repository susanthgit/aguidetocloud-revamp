## Who Should Take This Exam?

The PMI-CPMAI is designed for **professionals specialising in a focused domain**. Prior certification or equivalent experience recommended.

**Prerequisites:** No prior project management, technical or AI experience or certifications are required. Completion of the PMI-CPMAI Exam Prep Course is required to schedule and take the exam. ## Exam Domains & Weights

The PMI-CPMAI exam covers **5 domains**. Focus your study time based on the weights below — higher-weighted domains have more exam questions.

| Domain | Weight | Practice Qs |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Support Responsible and Trustworthy AI Efforts | 15% | 37 |
| Identify Business Needs and Solutions | 26% | 65 |
| Identify Data Needs | 26% | 65 |
| Manage AI Model Development and Evaluation | 16% | 40 |
| Operationalize AI Solution | 17% | 43 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **250** |

> 💡 **Study tip:** **Identify Business Needs and Solutions** and **Identify Data Needs** tie as the heaviest domains (26% each) — together they are over half the exam, so ground yourself there first: problem framing and personas, initial AI feasibility, risk assessment and ROI on one side; defining required data, identifying sources and data SMEs, gathering it, and checking privacy, compliance and access on the other. **Operationalize AI Solution** (17%) follows, covering deployment planning, model governance, solution metrics and transition. **Manage AI Model Development and Evaluation** (16%) rewards practice on model technique selection, QA/QC, training, and the data-quality and readiness go/no-go decisions. **Support Responsible and Trustworthy AI Efforts** (15%) is the smallest domain but is heavily tested on transparency, bias checks, regulatory compliance and audit-trail accountability.
