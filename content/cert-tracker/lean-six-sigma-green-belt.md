## Who should take this exam?

The Green Belt sits in the middle of the IASSC belt structure, above Yellow Belt and below Black Belt. It suits someone who leads or takes part in improvement projects and wants to be assessed on the full DMAIC cycle rather than one phase of it.

IASSC lists no prerequisites. Training is recommended but not required, and you do not need a Yellow Belt first.

Typical study time is four to eight weeks of part-time study.

## Domains and practice coverage

**IASSC publishes no percentage weights for this exam.** It states that the exam draws approximately 20 questions from each of the five Body of Knowledge sections, so all five DMAIC phases carry equal weight. This bank mirrors that exactly, at 50 questions per phase, with no editorial re-weighting.

| DMAIC phase | Approximate exam questions | Practice questions |
|-------------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| Define | 20 | 50 |
| Measure | 20 | 50 |
| Analyze | 20 | 50 |
| Improve | 20 | 50 |
| Control | 20 | 50 |
| **Total** | **100** | **250** |

Because every phase carries the same weight, there is no heaviest phase to prioritise and no phase that is safe to skip. Work through them in DMAIC order instead, since each phase assumes the outputs of the one before it.

Equal weighting does not mean equal breadth. The Improve section has far fewer sub-topics than Analyze, so the same number of questions is spread across much less material, which means Improve is probed more deeply per topic. This bank follows that same shape.

## PeopleCert certification path

IASSC certifies three belts: Yellow Belt (ICYB), Green Belt (ICGB) and Black Belt (ICBB). They are independent qualifications rather than a locked sequence, so you can sit the Green Belt without holding a Yellow Belt.

Choose the belt by the scope you are expected to work at. Yellow Belt covers the Define, Measure and Control phases for someone supporting an improvement project. Green Belt covers the full DMAIC cycle for someone running projects. Black Belt adds advanced statistical analysis and the leadership of larger programmes.
