## About the CSPP Exam

> Practise sustainable project decisions across P5 impacts, PRiSM delivery, ESG reporting and project governance.

This independent bank has **250 original revision questions** across the six domains in PMI's standard-path examination outline. It includes explanations, wrong-answer reasoning and public-source references. It is not official PMI or GPM material, required training, an exam voucher or an exact exam simulation.

The certified-practitioner path excludes Domain VI. This bank includes that domain, so practitioner-path candidates should distinguish it from their own examination scope. PMI-authorized training remains required.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

CSPP is for project professionals applying sustainability methods to project decisions.

**Prerequisites:** The standard bundle requires a secondary diploma and 20 hours of qualifying education. The practitioner bundle requires a qualifying active project-management credential or a project/program management master's degree and 12 hours. PMI's CSPP prep course or an authorized training partner is required before scheduling either exam. This revision bank does not satisfy that requirement.
