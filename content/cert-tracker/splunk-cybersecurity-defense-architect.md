## Who Should Take This Exam?

The Splunk Certified Cybersecurity Defense Architect certification is designed for **senior security engineers and SOC architects who already run Splunk Enterprise Security and Splunk SOAR in production** and are accountable for how detection, ingest and response are designed across the whole platform — not for operating a single dashboard. Expect scenario questions that ask you to choose and place a capability under a real architectural constraint.

**Prerequisites:** None required (5-7 years of security experience plus Splunk Enterprise Security and Splunk SOAR familiarity recommended)

## Splunk Certification Path

Splunk's security track builds on its core searching credentials. Start with the **Splunk Core Certified User** and **Splunk Core Certified Power User** (SPL, knowledge objects, data models, and the CIM), then earn the **Splunk Certified Cybersecurity Defense Analyst** (SOC operations, Enterprise Security, and Risk-Based Alerting) and the **Splunk Certified Cybersecurity Defense Engineer** (detection engineering, SOAR playbook automation, and the detection lifecycle). The **Splunk Certified Cybersecurity Defense Architect** (this exam — SPLK-5003) sits at the top of that track as the expert-level capstone, moving from operating and engineering detections to designing the platform they run on. Note that Splunk sets **no prerequisite exams** for this certification — the ladder above is a recommended progression, not a gate, and Splunk describes the typical candidate as having 5-7 years of security experience.
