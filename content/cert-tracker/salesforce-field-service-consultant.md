## About the Agentforce Field Service and Operations Consultant Exam

> Master the Salesforce Certified Agentforce Field Service and Operations Consultant exam: model service resources, crews and service territories against real operating hours and skills, configure work types and work order line items so a work order actually reflects the job, choose the schedule policy and the dispatch strategy that meet the business requirement, know when optimization beats manual dispatch and when aerial routing beats street-level, configure the Field Service mobile app for a technician who is offline, and get inventory, assets, maintenance plans and Field Service permission sets right the first time.

The complete 250-question practice exam for the Salesforce Certified Agentforce Field Service and Operations Consultant credential, written to the eight sections Salesforce publishes in its official exam guide. Covers managing resources including service resources and the crew model, resource types, service territories and territory members, the relationship between time sheets, time sheet entries, service resources and work orders, skills, skill levels and time-based skills, operating hours applied to resources, accounts, work orders and appointment booking, and the Field Service license types and when each is deployed; managing work orders including work order processes and parameters, work types, resource preferences, products required, work order line items, work order milestones, and the relationship between service appointments, work orders and work order line items; managing scheduling and optimization including field service settings, the service appointment lifecycle and the actions that move it, schedule policies and work rules and service objectives, complex work and multi-day service appointments, aerial versus street-level routing, dispatch strategy, Dispatcher Console filtering, setting up optimization, choosing between the scheduling and optimization services, and the DateTime tracking fields; configuring mobility including the customer sign-off process, configuring the Field Service mobile app to support a business process, the difference between the Field Service mobile app and the Salesforce mobile app, and the available mobile settings; managing inventory including price book models for products consumed, the applicable inventory model, and the return order object model and process; managing assets including asset records, asset hierarchies and asset relationships; configuring maintenance plans including choosing the right maintenance plan for a scenario; and permissions and sharing including the Field Service permission set types, how scheduled service appointments are shared with service resources, and the difference between user territories and service territories. Original practice questions.

## Salesforce Field Service Certification Path

The Salesforce Certified Agentforce Field Service and Operations Consultant sits deeper in the Salesforce stack than most credentials, because it is one of the few with hard prerequisites: you need both the [Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator](/cert-tracker/salesforce-administrator/) and the Salesforce Certified Agentforce Service Consultant before you can sit it. That ordering is the path. Platform Administrator establishes the org, the data model, sharing and Flow; the Agentforce Service Consultant credential establishes the service side — cases, channels and the service console; and this exam then adds the field dimension on top of both: resources, crews and territories, work orders and line items, the scheduling and optimization engines, the mobile app a technician actually uses offline, and the inventory, assets and maintenance plans that keep the work coming. The product was renamed from Field Service to Agentforce Field Service and Operations, so expect the newer wording in the official guide.
