## About the CAPM exam

CAPM introduces the language and decisions used in project work. It covers project fundamentals, predictive planning, agile approaches and business analysis. You need to recognise what a situation calls for, not just remember the name of a document or technique.

This independent practice bank follows PMI's **2023 CAPM Exam Content Outline**, checked against PMI's current materials in September 2026. It contains 250 original single-answer questions. These are not official PMI questions, recalled exam questions or copies of vendor samples.

**Check older study material against the current outline.** Business analysis has its own domain, and adaptive approaches are tested alongside predictive methods. A resource covering only a traditional project lifecycle will not cover the whole outline.

## Who is CAPM for?

CAPM is an entry-level credential for people starting a project-management career or contributing to project teams. Project-management experience is not required.

PMI requires a secondary qualification, a minimum age of 18 and **23 hours of project-management education completed before the exam**. Check [PMI's current CAPM eligibility requirements](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/certified-associate-capm) before paying for a course or booking an exam.

This practice bank is revision material. It provides **no qualifying education hours, exam voucher or certification**.

## Domains and practice coverage

| Domain | Official exam weight | Practice questions |
|--------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts | 36% | 90 |
| Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies | 17% | 43 |
| Agile Frameworks/Methodologies | 20% | 50 |
| Business Analysis Frameworks | 27% | 67 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **250** |

The 90/43/50/67 allocation rounds the official weights to 250 questions, giving the tied rounding remainder to the earlier domain. It does not change PMI's exam weights.

**Fundamentals** covers project, program, portfolio and operations distinctions; planning; roles; communication; risk; benefits; problem-solving; and professional conduct. For ethics, use [PMI's current Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/ethics/pmi-code-of-ethics.pdf), rather than treating any association's code as interchangeable with PMI's.

**Predictive methods** covers when a plan-based approach fits, work breakdown structures, work packages, schedules and project controls. Schedule variance in earned value is expressed in budget units, not a number of calendar days.

**Agile methods** covers adaptive suitability, iteration planning and tracking, framework components, controls and task priorities. Different frameworks have different roles and practices; Scrum terminology is not a universal definition of agile.

**Business analysis** covers stakeholder roles and communication, elicitation, requirements, traceability, backlogs, roadmaps, release planning and acceptance criteria. Collecting a requirement, tracing it and deciding whether a completed result meets it are related but different activities.

## Practice exam

[Try the CAPM practice questions](/guided/capm/practice/). Try **20 questions free**. Full access to this certification's **250-question bank costs US$9 for one year**.

Each question has four answer choices and one correct answer. It includes an explanation, a hint, reasons the other choices are wrong, a study tip, a practical note and a public-source reference.

**This is selected-response revision, not a full simulation of the CAPM exam.** PMI also describes drag-and-drop, animation or comic-strip, and hot-spot items. This bank uses written scenarios and answer choices; it does not reproduce those interactive formats.

Use the explanations to check what made the answer fit and why the alternatives did not. A practice result is not a guarantee of an official exam result.
