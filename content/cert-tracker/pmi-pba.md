## About the PMI-PBA Exam

> Practise business analysis decisions across needs assessment, planning, analysis, traceability and evaluation.

Independent original PMI-PBA concept and application practice aligned to PMI's five-domain Examination Content Outline, inspected September 2026. Four-option questions include explanations, wrong-answer reasoning and public-source references.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The PMI-PBA is designed for **experienced professionals seeking advanced validation**. 2+ years of hands-on experience recommended.

**Prerequisites:** PMI offers three eligibility routes, each requiring 35 contact hours of training in business analysis practices: a secondary degree with 60 months of business analysis experience, a bachelor's degree or higher with 36 months, or a bachelor's or postgraduate degree from a GAC accredited program with 24 months. The experience must have been earned in the last eight years. Check PMI's current eligibility rules before applying; this practice bank does not establish eligibility or provide qualifying training hours.

**Typical study time:** 8-12 weeks of intensive study

## Exam Domains & Weights

The PMI-PBA exam covers **5 domains**. Focus your study time based on the weights below — higher-weighted domains have more exam questions.

| Domain | Weight | Practice Qs |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Needs Assessment | 18% | 45 |
| Planning | 22% | 55 |
| Analysis | 35% | 88 |
| Traceability and Monitoring | 15% | 37 |
| Evaluation | 10% | 25 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **250** |

> 💡 **Study tip:** **Analysis** carries the most weight (35%) — start there. **Evaluation** has the least (10%), but don't skip it — exam questions can come from any domain.
