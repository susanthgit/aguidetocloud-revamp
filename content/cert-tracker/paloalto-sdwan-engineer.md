## About the SD-WAN Engineer Exam

> Design, deploy, operate, and troubleshoot Prisma SD-WAN from the ION edge to Strata Cloud Manager and Unified SASE.

The Palo Alto Networks Certified SD-WAN Engineer certification validates product-specific engineering skills across the Prisma SD-WAN lifecycle. It covers planning and design, ION deployment and site configuration, branch and data-center routing, VRF segmentation, policy-driven path selection, operations and monitoring, Unified SASE integration, and evidence-led troubleshooting. The exam expects you to understand how ION devices, AppFabric, stacked policies, WAN Clarity, ADEM, Cloud Identity Engine, Device-ID, User-ID, and Strata Copilot fit together in a real branch and data-center design.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

This is a **specialist-level** certification for SD-WAN and SASE engineers, network engineers and administrators, professional-services consultants, and support engineers who plan, deploy, operate, or troubleshoot Prisma SD-WAN.

Hands-on familiarity with routing, WAN design, BGP and OSPF, QoS, IPsec, network monitoring, and policy-driven traffic steering is useful. The exam is product-specific: generic SD-WAN knowledge is not enough without understanding Prisma SD-WAN objects, workflows, evidence, and current Strata Cloud Manager terminology.

**Typical study time:** 6-10 weeks of focused study

> **Study tip:** Keep the object boundaries clear. A **Circuit**, **Circuit Label**, and **Circuit Category** are different things. **Analytics mode** observes; **Control mode** joins the fabric and enforces policy. A routing failure, unhealthy path, and policy-selection problem can produce similar symptoms, so always prove the fault domain with site, flow, route, incident, and policy evidence before changing configuration.

## Study Tips

1. **Learn the Prisma SD-WAN object model** — ION device, site, circuit, Circuit Label, Circuit Category, WAN Network, interface, policy stack, and VRF context each solve a different problem
2. **Separate design from deployment** — choose the right topology and policy approach first, then learn the exact claim, site, interface, route, template, and Control-mode workflow
3. **Know policy order and evidence** — use Flow Details and policy lookup evidence to distinguish Path, QoS, Security, NAT, and Performance policy behavior
4. **Treat Unified SASE as an integration boundary** — understand the Prisma SD-WAN side of Prisma Access, ADEM, Device-ID, Cloud Identity Engine, and User-ID or Group-ID
5. **Troubleshoot from evidence** — start with incidents and site health, then inspect circuits, Secure Fabric links, flows, routes, policy matches, application metrics, WAN Clarity, and Strata Copilot guidance
6. **Use the practice exam** — try the 20 free questions first, then review every explanation and wrong-answer rationale
