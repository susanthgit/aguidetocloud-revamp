## About the AB-650 Exam

> Administer, govern, and secure Microsoft 365 tenants, Copilot, and AI agents (Microsoft's beta exam AB-650)

Microsoft 365 Certified: AI Services Administrator Associate (in beta as of August 2026). The admin cert for running Microsoft 365 in an AI-powered workplace — tenant and workload configuration, Purview information protection and retention, Defender XDR threat policies, and the newer AI control plane: Copilot deployment and readiness, Entra Agent ID, the agent registry, Microsoft Agent 365, and Copilot Cowork. Heavy on governance: Domain 2 alone is 40–45% of the paper.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The AB-650 is designed for **IT professionals with some hands-on experience**. 6-12 months of hands-on experience recommended.

**Typical study time:** 4-8 weeks of focused study
