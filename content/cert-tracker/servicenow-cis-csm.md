## Who Should Take This Exam?

CIS-CSM is for people who **configure, implement or maintain ServiceNow Customer Service Management**. Useful experience includes customer-service processes, customer and product records, case workflows, routing and the CSM application suite.

**Prerequisites:** Recommended CSM training and hands-on implementation experience; consult the current ServiceNow registration requirements.

**Preparation:** Work through the official objectives and practise the tasks you have not yet used. The blueprint recommends training and practical experience; it does not describe CSA certification as a mandatory entry requirement.

## ServiceNow Certification Path

CIS-CSM focuses on implementing Customer Service Management rather than general platform administration or another product's workflows. ServiceNow recommends training including Administration Fundamentals, Customer Service Management Essentials, Knowledge Management for CSM, and CSM Best Practices for Implementation. Follow the current CIS-CSM registration requirements; do not assume that another specialist credential's prerequisites apply here.
