## Who is this exam for?

This certification is for developers who build and manage AI apps and agents with Python and Microsoft Foundry. You should have experience developing Python applications and be familiar with Azure and generative AI.

---

## Skills Measured

The [official skills outline](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-103) covers planning and managing Azure AI solutions, generative AI and agents, computer vision, text analysis, and information extraction.

The time at the top of this page estimates reading **our 27 lessons**, not completing Microsoft's training or becoming exam-ready. Microsoft's four self-paced paths are separate resources:

| Microsoft Learn path | Published learning time, checked 31 October 2026 |
|---|---|
| [Develop generative AI apps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/develop-generative-ai-apps/) | 6h 52m |
| [Develop AI agents](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/develop-ai-agents-azure/) | 9h 52m |
| [Develop natural language solutions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/develop-language-solutions-azure-ai/) | 5h 46m |
| [Extract insights from visual data](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/insight-visual-data/) | 7h 6m |

Those published path durations add up to **29h 36m**. They are learning-time estimates, not a promise of total exam preparation time. Allow additional time for practice, revision, and topics you find unfamiliar; Microsoft can update these paths.
