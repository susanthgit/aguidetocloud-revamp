## About the ServiceNow CIS-HR Exam

> Master the ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Human Resources exam: work out which application scope an HR table actually lives in before you try to write to it, tell an HR Service apart from the HR Catalog Item and the Record Producer that opens it, follow an HR case from the Employee Center request through the assignment rule that routes it to the right COE, decide whether a user sees a knowledge article because of User Criteria or because of an ACL, and know exactly how far the Delegated Developer role lets someone go before contextual security stops them reading HR data they should never see.

A full-length 235-question practice exam for the ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist – Human Resources (CIS-HR) credential, written to the three-domain blueprint ServiceNow publishes in KB0011654 (v8.0, the latest version). Covers the ServiceNow HR System Architecture including the plugins that install the HR suite, the HR table structure and how the HR tables extend and relate to one another, and application scoping across Human Resources: Core, Integrations, Lifecycle Events and Service Portal; configuring and implementing the core HR applications and Employee Center including users, groups, skills and roles, assignment rules and how an HR case reaches the right fulfiller, HR Services, HR Catalog Items and Record Producers and how a COE relates to an HR Service, and Employee Center branding and theming; and platform, role and contextual security including how platform security, role-based access and contextual security combine on the Now Platform, how the Delegated Developer role works and what it deliberately cannot do, and how to configure security options — User Criteria, HR Criteria, Access Controls and HR profile protections — to keep HR data visible only to the people entitled to see it. Grounded in ServiceNow's own product documentation for the current release family.

## Who Should Take This Exam?

The CIS-HR is designed for **implementation specialists who configure and support ServiceNow HR Service Delivery** — the people who install and scope the HR applications, model HR Services and HR Catalog Items, wire the assignment rules that route a case to the right COE, brand Employee Center, and set the User Criteria, HR Criteria and Access Controls that decide who can see an HR record. ServiceNow states recommended experience here rather than enforcing an entry requirement.

**Prerequisites:** None required — ServiceNow publishes only recommended experience (three to six months of ServiceNow deployment or instance-maintenance experience, plus participation in at least one ServiceNow HR deployment project)

**Typical study time:** 6-10 weeks of focused study

## ServiceNow Certification Path

ServiceNow does **not** gate this credential — there is no certification you must already hold before registering for CIS-HR. In practice most candidates still start with the **Certified System Administrator (CSA)**, because CIS-HR assumes you can already work the platform underneath HR: navigation and instance configuration, lists and forms, the data schema and access control, Service Catalog and Workflow Studio, and update sets. ServiceNow's own entry advice is three to six months of deployment or instance-maintenance experience plus participation in at least one HR deployment project — experience, not an exam.
