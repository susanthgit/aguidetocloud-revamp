## Who Should Take This Exam?

The CIS-RC is designed for **implementation specialists who configure ServiceNow Governance, Risk and Compliance** — the people who scope the entity framework, model policies and controls, run control attestations and indicators, wire continuous monitoring and regulatory change, and take an audit engagement through its lifecycle. ServiceNow states recommended experience here rather than enforcing an entry requirement.

**Prerequisites:** No prerequisite certification is required for CIS-RC. ServiceNow recommends the Risk and Compliance implementation training and hands-on implementation experience; consult the current ServiceNow registration requirements.

**Typical study time:** 6-10 weeks of focused study
