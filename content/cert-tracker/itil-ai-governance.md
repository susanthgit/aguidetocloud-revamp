## About ITIL AI Governance (Version 5)

> Prepare for ITIL AI Governance (Version 5) with original scenarios on AI benefits and risks, good AI governance, the ITIL AI Capability Model, the ITIL AI Governance Improvement Model, how regulation shapes AI governance, and integrating AI governance with other frameworks.

This independent practice bank follows PeopleCert's published learning outcomes for the ITIL AI Governance (Version 5) module. Its 250 original questions cover those outcomes. They are not real exam questions, and no vendor sample questions or text from any ITIL core publication is reproduced.

**Check the version before studying.** ITIL Version 5 is a different syllabus from ITIL 4. This module sits alongside the ITIL Value System, which replaces ITIL 4's Service Value System, so material written for ITIL 4 will not prepare you for it.

## Who should take this exam?

ITIL AI Governance (Version 5) is a standalone extension module. PeopleCert lists no prerequisites for it, and it does not require ITIL Foundation, so you can take it without holding any other ITIL qualification.
